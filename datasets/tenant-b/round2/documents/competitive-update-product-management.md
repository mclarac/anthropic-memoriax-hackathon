# Competitive update from Tidewell product management, 8 October 2026

**Carepath Remote** announced a bundled offering for health systems on Epic: devices, a licensed nurse triage line staffed 24/7, and no per-patient fee in year one for systems that commit to three years. It has HITRUST r2 certification.

What it means for Ashgrove: the triage line addresses the nurse-workload objection directly and the certification addresses the new security objection. Expect Carepath to lead with both. The year-one free pricing is real but the 3-year commit and the triage line's escalation rules (it escalates to the on-call physician, not to Ashgrove's transitional care nurses) are the gaps.

**Our response:**
- CareLoop adds an optional partner triage line from 1 November 2026; Ashgrove nurses remain first-line by default.
- HITRUST r2 interim: Tidewell has completed the readiness assessment and can share the gap list under NDA. The certification report is expected in Q1 2027; do not state a date to the customer.
- Pilot pricing stays per enrolled patient with no multi-year commit.
