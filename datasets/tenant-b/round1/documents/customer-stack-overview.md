# Ashgrove Regional Health: clinical and IT overview

Source: two calls with the population health team and one with IT, notes by Rui Santos.

Ashgrove is a four-hospital system with eleven ambulatory clinics in rural and small-city counties, about 38,000 inpatient discharges a year and 2,100 employed physicians and advanced practice providers.

## Systems
- Epic is the EHR (Hyperspace 2025 release), with MyChart in use by roughly 58 percent of active patients.
- Integration engine: Rhapsody, run by a four-person interface team under Dr. Anil Rao (CMIO) and the CIO's office.
- Existing RPM: a small pilot with a different vendor, 140 patients, ended in March because of unreliable cellular pairing in two counties.
- Identity is Okta; clinical alerts reach nurses through Epic In Basket and Vocera badges.

## The clinical problem
- Heart failure 30-day readmission rate is 23.4 percent against a national figure the team cites as about 20 percent.
- Ashgrove's estimated CMS Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program penalty exposure is about 1.9 million dollars a year.
- Transitional care nurses (9 FTE) call patients after discharge but can reach only about a third of the discharged heart failure cohort in the first week.
- Roughly 40 percent of the HF cohort lives more than 30 minutes from an Ashgrove clinic.
