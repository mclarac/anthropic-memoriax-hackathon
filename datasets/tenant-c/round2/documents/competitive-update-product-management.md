# Competitive update from Harrowgate product management, 12 October 2026

**Ironvale Condition Monitoring** released a wireless battery-powered sensor line with on-premises-only analytics. Sensors stick to the housing with no cabling, so installation does not need an outage; a technician can fit about 40 sensors in a shift while the machine runs. Battery life is quoted at 3 years.

What it means for Doverfield: it directly answers the outage-window objection and the OT-security objection. Expect Ironvale to say "install this week, no shutdown". Its limits: wireless sampling is lower-rate than our wired sensors, which matters for early gearbox fault detection, and it has no motor-current analysis or SAP PM integration at launch.

**Our response:**
- Harrowgate field services will supply installation technicians and a fixed-price install with an overrun guarantee for the February outage.
- A phased install plan: PM3 dryer section drives first, 48 sensors in a short stop, remainder at outage.
- Do not call Ironvale's wireless sensors inaccurate; say sampling rate is lower and describe which faults need higher rates.
